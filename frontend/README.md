# Kayak Trip Planner

A web application for planning, running, and reviewing multi-day kayak expeditions.

The app supports two main phases of an expedition: **preparation** — building the itinerary, assembling the crew, managing gear and logistics — and the **live expedition** itself — daily check-ins, real-time conditions, waypoints, notes, and photos. After the trip, everything is preserved as a read-only log with experience tracking and crew endorsements.

Users can also discover and share routes with the wider community, and invite external viewers (family, followers) to follow along during an active expedition.

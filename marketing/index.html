A route, a crew, a plan the weather can't surprise.

Kayaktrek is a route planner built specifically for multi-day sea kayak trips — tides, weather, float plans, and the people paddling with you, in one place instead of six.

What you get

Built for the planning a real expedition needs, not a generic trip itinerary.

  • Map-first route building

    Drop points on a chart and get per-day distance, tide gates, and paddle time — not a form to fill in blind.

  • Weather and sea state, per day

    Forecasts and tidal streams pulled in automatically for each leg of the trip, not looked up separately after the plan is done.

  • A float plan someone can actually use

    Share it by link with a shore contact who has no account and no app to install.

  • One plan, the whole crew

    Crew invitations, shared logistics, and a packing list everyone can see and contribute to.

  • Live position while you're out

    Share where you are during the trip with the people waiting to hear that you're back.

  • A gear list that's actually tracked

    Personal kit and trip-shared inventory, with who's bringing what — not a paper checklist nobody updates.

  • Paddlers you actually trust

    Connect with the people you paddle with, invite them straight into a trip, and block who you don't.

  • A logbook that means something

    Track your own paddling history and get it endorsed by the people who were actually there.

About

Kayaktrek is built by one paddler who kept re-planning the same trip across a chart, a tide table, a weather app, and a group chat, and got tired of it. It's a small, early product — this is its first public release — built and run by one person, not a company with a support team behind it.

Trip data is private by default. Nothing about a trip, a crew, or a route is visible to anyone else unless you choose to share it — a public route, a float-plan link, a trip story.
